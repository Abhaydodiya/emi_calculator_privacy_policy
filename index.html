Privacy Policy

EMI Calculator · Last updated: 15 October 2026

In short: EMI Calculator does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the EMI Calculator app ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is published by [YOUR NAME] ("the developer").

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, sign-in, phone number, email address or any other personal detail.

The app does not collect:

  • Your name, contact details or identifiers
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, files or microphone/camera data
  • Device advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

3. Data stored on your device

The loan details you enter (such as amount, interest rate, tenure and extra payments) are used only to calculate results on your device. If you choose to save a loan, it is stored in the app's local storage on your device, together with your theme and language preferences. This data is never sent to us or to any third party. You can delete saved loans inside the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data.

4. Exporting and sharing

If you export a payment schedule as PDF or CSV, the file is created on your device. It is shared only when you choose to send it, using your device's standard share sheet, and only to the app or person you pick. We do not receive a copy.

5. Internet access and third parties

The app's calculations work fully offline. It does not connect to our servers and does not include advertising, analytics or tracking software from third parties. Because no data is collected, none is sold, rented or shared.

6. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose financial calculator and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's own protections, such as a screen lock and operating-system updates.

8. Not financial advice

Results shown in the app are estimates based on the figures you enter. Actual EMI, interest and charges may differ by lender, and may include fees, taxes or rounding that the app does not account for. The app is a calculation tool, does not offer loans, and does not provide financial advice.

9. Your rights

As we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. If you have a question about your data, contact us using the details below.
